## Task scope

- Carry a focused change through implementation, checks and review. A few hundred lines inside one module is a normal task.
- File counts, failing tests and layout defects alone are not reasons to stop; fix and verify them.
- Stop and ask when the work would break existing callers, change a module's responsibility, or need a coordinated redesign of several modules.
- Keep useful partial work as a draft labelled unfinished, with its checks and remaining issues.
- An owner's hold or request to wait stands until the owner lifts it. Review bots and CI never resume held work.
